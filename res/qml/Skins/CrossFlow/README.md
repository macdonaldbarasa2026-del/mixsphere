CrossFlow is an original, Cross DJ–*inspired* performance skin for the Mixxx
Android fork. It gives the fork the landscape two-deck layout of the earlier
Mixsphere spike (waveform strip, decks, a mode rail, a swapping mode panel and
a crossfader bar) while driving the **real Mixxx engine** through
`Mixxx.ControlProxy` — not a mock audio engine.

It does not copy Cross DJ artwork, colors, fonts or branding. All icons in
`icons/` are original SVGs drawn for this skin. The *arrangement* is modeled on
a phone DJ surface — waveforms across the top, large jog wheels under them, a
narrow mode rail, and a crossfader bar at the bottom — with jog diameters given
most of the available height. Cross DJ's own layouts and assets cannot be
copied: they are proprietary and are not part of this repository.

## Look and feel

The palette and proportions follow a Cross DJ–style phone surface using
original values and drawings: `#2b2d35` panels, `#1d1d1d` sunken areas,
`#4a4d53` borders, vinyl-groove rings and bend arrows on the jog wheels, 6-pad
sampler banks (1/2/3) and a 48dp-style REC / auto-fade crossfader bar. Deck A is
blue and deck B is orange to keep them readable at a glance.

## Graceful exit

Pressing back/close asks for confirmation (the app-level dialog). When the app
actually quits, the skin stops both decks, cancels any running stem separation
jobs, and saves the current decks, sampler tracks, crossfader and selected mode
to a small local SQLite session (via `QtQuick.LocalStorage`). They are restored
the next time the skin loads. Nothing is copied to a remote server.

## Build and select

1. Build the Android fork with QML enabled (see `packaging/android/README.md`).
   Skins need no CMake change: the whole `res/qml` tree is copied to
   `assets/qml`, and new skins are discovered automatically by
   `Mixxx.MobileApp.skinsIn()`.
2. Install the APK, then open **Settings → Interface → Skin** and choose
   **CrossFlow**. The skin is selected immediately and remembered in
   `[Config] AndroidSkin`.
3. If the skin fails to load, the app falls back to Classic, so you are never
   locked out.

Loading a sample: hold a sampler pad (or use the library track menu
**Load to → Sampler**) and pick a WAV/MP3. Stem separation is triggered from
the STEM panel or the shared library popup; progress and the "stems ready"
notice appear in the top bar and in `Skin.StemsOverlay`.
